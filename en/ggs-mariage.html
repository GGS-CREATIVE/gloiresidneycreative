FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a wedding with GGS Creative cost?
Packages start at €1,400 for 12 hours of coverage with one photographer or one videographer. The final price depends on the team and deliverables: I send you a detailed quote after we talk.
What if the day lasts more than 12 hours?
Each extra hour beyond 12 hours is charged €100.
Do you cover traditional weddings?
Yes. Dowry and traditional ceremonies are one of my specialities, alongside civil and religious weddings. For a traditional ceremony only, I offer a dedicated package on quote.
How do I book my date?
Send me your date and venues. Once the quote is approved, a 50% deposit secures the date.
How do I receive my photos?
In an online gallery that is easy to share with your loved ones. The Prestige package also includes an album.
